Our Understanding of Casino Games and Slots

Big Bass covers online slots and casino games from an editorial and technical perspective. We focus on the mechanics that actually affect how a game plays rather than relying on promotional descriptions.


When reviewing or explaining slots, we consider:


  • RTP: the theoretical return to player percentage over a very large number of spins, not a prediction for an individual session;
  • volatility: whether a game is designed around frequent smaller wins, less frequent larger wins, or a balance between the two;
  • paylines and ways to win: how winning combinations are formed and what the player is paying for per spin;
  • bonus features: free spins, multipliers, collect symbols, expanding wilds, respins, pick bonuses and gamble features;
  • bet ranges: minimum and maximum stakes, and how these interact with bonus terms;
  • game pace: autoplay, turbo modes and features that can increase the speed of play;
  • provider information: the studio behind the game and whether the game is supplied through recognised casino software channels.

We also cover table games and live casino products where useful, but slots remain a major part of our editorial focus because they are widely available and often misunderstood.
